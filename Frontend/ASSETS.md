# TrustSphere visual assets

The existing page layouts, colors, and component structure are retained. The assets in `Frontend/public/images/testimonials/` replace unrelated template illustrations.

## AI artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Source PNGs are saved in the project as `hero-light.png` and `hero-dark.png`. Optimized WebP variants are used on the site. These are conceptual illustrations, not product screenshots or real customer portraits.

Light prompt:

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a polished website hero illustration for TrustSphere, a text and video testimonial collection product. Landscape 3:2 composition. A beautifully composed floating cluster of three rounded testimonial cards, one with a friendly stylized 3D clay person inside a video thumbnail and a circular play icon, one with a large blue quotation mark and five warm gold stars, one with three small stylized diverse avatar circles and a mint green checkmark. The cards sit around a blue and mint speech bubble symbol. Premium restrained 3D editorial illustration with soft shadows, porcelain white cards, cobalt blue #3068F5, mint #7DE6C3, lavender accents. Background solid very pale cool gray #F4F7FF. Generous breathing room at edges, cohesive centered composition, crisp render, not a dashboard screenshot. Communicate real customer stories, text reviews, video testimonials. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos of other companies, no Slack, Gmail, analytics charts or watermarks. This is illustrative sample content, not a real customer endorsement.

Dark edit prompt (using the light artwork as the reference):

Edit this TrustSphere testimonial illustration for the dark theme of the same website. Preserve the exact composition, characters, objects, five gold stars, speech bubbles and geometry. Change the pale background to uniform midnight navy #111322, change the white card surfaces to slate navy #252A42 with subtle lighter edges, keep faces warmly lit and cobalt blue, mint and lavender accents luminous but restrained. No text or new objects. Maintain landscape 3:2 format.

## Vector artwork

The TrustSphere wordmarks, favicon, collection/inbox/wall illustrations, example avatars, and workflow icons are original project-local SVG assets. UI illustrations are labelled as examples and do not represent real submissions. Light and dark variants share the same geometry.

## Editorial content

The testimonials page uses explicitly labelled fictional examples until real customer endorsements are available. No customer names, results, usage figures, or endorsements have been invented as factual claims. Blog articles are original introductory guides. No external stock images or third-party brand endorsements are used in the new assets.
